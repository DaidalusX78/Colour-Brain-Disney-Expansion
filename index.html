How to Play

Quick Tabletop Guide & Official Rules

1

Hold Your 11 Colour Cards

Each player or team starts with a hand of the 11 official Colour Brain cards:

2

Check the Mickey Number & Pick Cards

Read the question aloud. The number inside the Mickey silhouette in the bottom-right corner tells you how many colour cards (1, 2, or 3) to play face-down on the table.

3

Reveal, Flip & Tap Winners

Everyone reveals their physical colour cards simultaneously! Tap the card (or ANSWER) to flip it, tap the score boxes at the top for every team that got all required colours right, then press CONFIRM SCORES.

4

How Scoring Works (First to 10 Wins!)

• Some teams right: Each correct team scores +1 pt (or wins the pot!).

• Everyone right OR Nobody right: 0 pts awarded! (Tip: Enable Pot Rollover in the menu if you want 0-pt rounds to roll over into a jackpot!)
